When times come out

Pick the day you want to play, and each course shows whether its times for that day are out, or when they should come out. We work this out from when we’ve seen each course put up new times before, so it gets better over a few weeks.

    Times are local to each course. We read most courses’ next week about every half hour, and dates further out every 6 hours or so. A release on those later dates is only known to within a few hours, so those aren’t used for the usual time. Courses on TeeItUp are read once an hour. We read 4 weeks ahead. For courses whose times already go that far, each run also peeks at the next few days past their last date, up to about 8 weeks out, to catch the day they put up the next one.
