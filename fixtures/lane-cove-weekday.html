Welcome to Lane Cove Golf Club


Golf course access through our main entrance has now reopened.

Lane Cove Golf Club is an nine hole public course located at the Galuwa Recreation Centre, 180 River Road, Lane Cove.

This challenging course (par 60 with a composite of par 3 and par 4 holes) is located on 15 hectares of undulating land along Gore Creek.

Online bookings can be made up to 14 days in advance. If you need assistance, please call the golf shop on (02) 8360 6666.

All online bookings require payment at the time of booking. Customers can cancel any booking and receive a full refund up to 12 hours prior to your tee time.
